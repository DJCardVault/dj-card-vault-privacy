DJ Card Vault privacy notice

Updated November 12, 2026

DJ Card Vault is a private tool used by its owner to manage a trading-card business. It is not a public marketplace or a service that registers other sellers.

Information and purpose

The dashboard stores inventory, sales, shipping, and expense information supplied through the owner's Google Sheet. The eBay connection requests access to order and financial information for sales reconciliation. Connecting eBay stores authorization credentials; automatic sales imports are not yet enabled. A connection test reads an order response to confirm access without saving its contents.

Credentials and access

eBay sign-in happens on eBay. DJ Card Vault does not receive the seller's eBay password. Client credentials and access and refresh tokens are encrypted in server storage. The dashboard is restricted to its owner. The public notification endpoint does not display dashboard records or stored notifications.

Service providers

The tool uses eBay APIs, Google Sheets, and OpenAI Sites hosting and its infrastructure providers to operate. GitHub Pages hosts this public privacy notice. Information is used for business operations and is not sold or used for advertising by this application.

Retention and deletion

Inventory and financial records remain until the owner removes them, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. Disconnecting eBay in the dashboard deletes the saved access and refresh tokens; application credentials remain for reconnection. Authorization can also be revoked in eBay account settings. Account-deletion notifications are stored privately pending signature verification and processing. That processing integration is still being completed before automatic order imports are enabled.

Contact

For questions or requests regarding information held by DJ Card Vault, contact DJ Card Vault through eBay's seller-contact feature. Include the relevant transaction reference, but do not send passwords or authorization tokens.
